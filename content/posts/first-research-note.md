# Knowledge first, design decisions next

Engineering design depends on more than a prediction model. Before selecting a geometry or optimization method, engineers need to interpret requirements, operating conditions, assumptions, and existing evidence.

## Why document-grounded AI?

Design manuals and technical databooks contain valuable facts, but the relevant information is often scattered across paragraphs, tables, and figures. A language model should be able to find **traceable evidence** before suggesting a design choice.

## Research direction

- Compare vector RAG and graph-based retrieval for engineering questions.
- Preserve relationships between requirements, configurations, and performance quantities.
- Connect retrieved design constraints with physically grounded predictive models.
